# Start here

The user supplies an idea once. The coordinator cleans the input, asks one follow-up if vague, then dispatches a Video Summary to Team TikTok, Team IG and Team Youtube. Each group contains the coordinator and four specialists working in order. Each lead posts one platform-ready block in that group, with fenced fields you can copy. Ask for edits there; only the lead replies. The coordinator logs CTAs, checks quality and sends one all-delivered message. A missing team after five minutes falls back to the coordinator.

SAME mode uses one video and identical on-screen text for all platforms. DIFFERENT mode routes each video's summary only to the appropriate team. The compatibility header TONIGHT'S VIDEO SUMMARY means today's video, regardless of check-in time.

The daily routine uses your local schedule. It checks in once, then sends at most one nudge and one ready-to-film idea if you have not replied. After that it stays quiet. Reply go to accept an offered idea; your own new idea takes priority. History starts empty. No other person is notified about silent days.

IG Thumbnail is a standalone bot: send a real frame (optional) and one sentence; choose a direction; receive a 1080×1920 JPEG. Without a frame it makes a text-only cover. It never redraws your face or body. Your signature is optional.

You film, choose edits and publish manually. No social login, publishing, outreach or payment is implied. The operator role defaults to you and approves material configuration changes. Comments/research and analytics are optional later phases, not mandatory elapsed-time milestones.

Begin with the [setup skill](skills/setup-content-engine/SKILL.md) or [deployment guide](02_DEPLOYMENT_GUIDE.md). Configure once, regenerate when context changes, and reinstall affected instructions. The resolved master wins over old chat memory. Existing editorial limits are engine defaults, not claims about platform algorithms or account eligibility.
