# Illustrative edits and failure handling

- Vague reply “a project”: coordinator asks one follow-up about what viewers should feel or learn.
- DIFFERENT reply: “TikTok is a checklist; IG and YouTube explain another process.” Coordinator creates the appropriate separate summaries and never sends the TikTok topic to the other teams.
- “Caption shorter” in Team IG: only its lead responds, reposting the caption+hashtags block under an updated header. Changed CTA produces a private edit log, not another initial delivery.
- No predecessor pack for ~60 seconds: the next specialist supplies it. No final block/log from a team five minutes after dispatch: coordinator drafts that platform’s four roles and posts once, marking Notes: drafted by the coordinator.
- No user reply: scheduled nudge and one idea only; then private silent-day log, no extra messages or notifications to another person.
- Unknown statistics, handles or eligibility: state unknown; never borrow example values.
